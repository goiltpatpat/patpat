# Preserve Safety

**Rule:** Never weaken auth, authorization, approvals, feature gates, default-off behavior, route guards, allowlists, boundary validation, sanitization, redaction, data-loss protection, rollback paths, public contracts, billing checks, or safety tests. Never expose plaintext secrets in prompts, logs, docs, diffs, or external calls.

**Test:** Before any destructive or externally consequential action, pause with blast radius, safer alternative, rollback path, and verification plan, and continue only with explicit authority.
